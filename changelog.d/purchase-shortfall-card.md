### Added

- Landing on a free city or beach you cannot afford now opens a card that says
  so: it names the price, your cash and what you are short of, and offers
  "Pass" as its main button. Beaches used to show a card with no choice and a
  dead confirmation; cities showed five locked levels and nothing to confirm.
  The same card appears when no building work on your own city fits your cash.
